Frequently Asked

Your questions, Answered

How much does it cost to rent a car?

Rental prices vary based on car type, duration and season. Our rates start from Ksh. 2000/day for compact cars and go upto Ksh. 4000/day for luxury vehicles. Weekly and monthly discounts are available.

What do I need to rent a car?

You'll need a valid driver's license and a copy of your National ID or Passport. International renters may need an International Driving Permit along with their license.

Can I rent a car for someone else?

The person renting a car must be present at pickup with their license and copy of National ID or Passport. However, you can add additional drivers to the rental agreement at a small fee.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation upto 48 hours before pickup. Cancellation within 48 hours may incur a fee. No-show are charged the full rental amount.

Is insurance included in the rental?

Third-party insurance is included in all rentals. Additional coverage options like theft protection are availabe at checkout.

Can I return a car to a different location?

Yes, one-way rentals are available between most locations. A one-way fee may apply depending on the distance between pickup and drop-off.
